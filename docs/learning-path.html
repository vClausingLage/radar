Tier 0 Maths you need before any radar makes sense

None of this is radar. All of it is what radar is written in.

TopicWhy radar needs itIn this repo
Sine, cosine, unit circle, radians vs degrees A radar signal is a cosine. Bearings, beams, phase — all of it is angles. Phaser.Math.DegToRad, Angle.WrapDegrees, every Math.cos/sin in antenna.ts and emitter.ts
Vectors, dot product, projection Line of sight, "how much hull faces me", closing velocity. crossSection() in signalPath.ts projects the hull across the line of sight
Exponents, logarithms, decibels Radar spans twelve or more orders of magnitude; everything is quoted in dB. The R⁴ law, the −3 dB beamwidth, 13 dB SNR. RADAR_TWO_WAY_RANGE_POWER = 4, Beer–Lambert Math.exp(-opticalDepth)
Complex numbers, Euler's formula ejθ = cos θ + j sin θ I/Q data, phase, Doppler and phased-array steering are all complex arithmetic. the gap between the game and a real signal chain
Probability: random variables, PDFs, Gaussian, Rayleigh / exponential Noise is random; detection is a probability; RCS fluctuates. detectionProbability(), isDetected(), decoySettings.BLOCK_PROBABILITY
Statistics: mean, variance, least squares Thresholds, filters, course fitting. reportCourse() is a least-squares slope; trimmedCentroid() is a robust mean

Tier 1 Waves and signals

What a pulse is, what happens to it in flight, and how fast you have to look at it.

TopicKey ideaIn this repo
Wavelength, frequency, speed of light; c·τ/2 Range = time of flight × c / 2. Pulse length sets range resolution. TERRAIN_PULSE_LENGTH_PX — the only place range resolution exists
Superposition, interference, phase Two waves add, constructively or destructively. This is the whole trick behind phased arrays and why RCS is lobed. not yet
Fourier transform (a signal is a sum of sines) Frequency content, Doppler, the matched filter, why bandwidth equals resolution. not yet
Sampling theorem (Nyquist), aliasing How fast you must sample. PRF is the Doppler sample rate; the sweep period is the track sample rate. Sweep = 60 frames = 1 s; TRACK_GATE_RADIUS_PX is really an anti-aliasing guard on target motion
Doppler effect Frequency shift ∝ radial velocity. Lets the radar see motion and reject stationary clutter. tracks get velocity only by differencing positions

Tier 2 The radar itself

The radar equation and everything it implies: how far, how sure, how fine.

TopicKey ideaIn this repo
The radar equation Pr = Pt·G²·λ²·σ / ((4π)³·R⁴). Every term is a lever. returnSignal() — folded into a normalised (R₀/R)⁴ · σ · T²
RCS (reflectivity) σ is not size but how much you scatter back. Shape, material, aspect, frequency. Flat plates glint, spheres are constant, stealth is shaping. crossSection() measures projected width only
Noise, noise floor, SNR kTBF. Thermal noise sets what you can hear; everything is relative to it. RWR_NOISE_FLOOR; a signal ratio of 1 is the floor
Detection theory: threshold, Pd, Pfa, Neyman–Pearson You choose Pfa, the threshold follows, Pd is what your SNR buys. Marcum / Swerling curves. Swerling I: detectionProbability = Pfa^(1/(1+signal)), RADAR_PFA
Pulse integration (coherent / non-coherent) N pulses on target → SNR gain of N (coherent) or about √N. Dwell time is why STT sees further than a sweep. implicit only: a big hull collects more ray hits per sweep
Resolution vs accuracy Resolution separates two targets (pulse length in range, beamwidth × R in angle). Accuracy locates one (improves with SNR). Different things. accuracy: measurementJitterPx() scales with 1/√(2·signal); resolution still isn't wired for ship contacts — TRACK_CLUSTER_RADIUS_PX stands in for it
Ambiguities: unambiguous range, PRF, blind speeds High PRF gives clean Doppler but ambiguous range; low PRF the reverse; medium PRF juggles. not yet
Pulse compression / matched filter / chirp A long pulse for energy and a wide bandwidth for resolution — have both. not yet

Tier 3 Antennas

Where the beam comes from, how wide it is, and how it gets pointed.

TopicKey ideaIn this repo
Aperture, gain, beamwidth (θ ≈ λ/D), sidelobes A bigger dish means a narrower beam and more gain. Sidelobes leak energy everywhere. beam is a single ray; STT_BEAM_DEG = 6; no sidelobes
Mechanical scan: gimbals, slew rate, scan pattern The dish must physically move, so it lags. Antenna.trackTo(), ANTENNA_SLEW_RATE_DEG_PER_SEC — well modelled
Phased array / AESA Steer by phase, not motion: instant pointing, several beams, interleaved modes. Cost: gain falls as cos θ off boresight, the beam broadens. the antenna model is mechanical
Monopulse angle tracking How STT measures angle finer than the beamwidth from a single pulse. the STT ray fan + trimmed centroid is a crude monopulse

Tier 4 Signal and data processing

Turning echoes into detections, and detections into tracks.

TopicKey ideaIn this repo
Clutter: ground / sea / volume, clutter-to-noise Unwanted echoes that raise the floor locally. roadmap item in TO_DO.md
MTI / Doppler filtering Reject zero-Doppler clutter; the notch; beaming a radar to hide in it. the beaming TODO is about RCS aspect, not Doppler
CFAR (CA, GO, SO, OS) Adaptive threshold from neighbouring cells so Pfa stays constant in clutter. Failure modes: target masking, clutter edges. fixed threshold
Track initiation (M-of-N), association, gating When is a blip a track? Which blip belongs to which track? TrackingComputer.update() — gate on predicted position, confidence
α-β and Kalman filters Smooth position, estimate velocity, predict. TRACK_FILTER_ALPHA / BETA — a real α-β filter
RWS, TWS, STT Sweep and track many coarsely, or stare at one finely. updateRws(), updateStt()

Tier 5 Electronic warfare and the rest of the loop

Most of this the game already has as gameplay. The learning here is understanding why each one works.

TopicKey ideaIn this repo
RWR / ESM One-way propagation: being swept is detectable far beyond being seen. Sidelobes make it worse for the emitter. emissionSignal(), rwr.ts, illuminateRwr()
Noise vs deceptive jamming (RGPO / VGPO) Drown the receiver, or feed it a plausible lie that walks away from the truth. jammer.ts does a range-and-angle pull-off; Receiver.createFakeHits()
Chaff and decoys A cloud of reflectors that hides the target behind returns of its own. Receiver.isBlockedByDecoy(), decoySettings
EMCON The cheapest countermeasure is not transmitting. 'emcon' mode
SARH / ARH seekers, datalinks Who illuminates the target, and what the missile is told on the way. fireControl.ts, missileRadar.ts, missileGuidance.ts, the VIM-220 waypoint link
Propagation losses Atmosphere, rain, and here: gas. Beer–Lambert attenuation along the path. gasTransmission()
IRST and passive sensors Sensing without emitting. roadmap item in TO_DO.md

How to study it

Sources, in the order they pay off

  • Marshall Bruner (YouTube, @MarshallBrunerRF) — the on-ramp for Tiers 1–4. Watch in this order: Radar Equation → Reflectivity → Signal-to-Noise Ratio → Sampling → Resolution → Phased Array → CFAR. Each one maps to a section of docs/realism-roadmap.md.
  • Christian Wolff, radartutorial.eu — free and encyclopaedic, one concept per page. Use it as the reference you open when a video term is unfamiliar.
  • MATLAB "Understanding Radar Principles" / "Radar Signal Processing" playlists — the maths with pictures, once the intuition is there.
  • One book, if you want one: Richards, Scheer & Holm, Principles of Modern Radar: Basic Principles — Tiers 2–4 in order, with the derivations.

Working method

For every concept, find the row's code pointer and read that function with the concept in mind. Where the third column says not modelled yet, the corresponding roadmap section says what the code would have to grow — implementing it is the exam.

Keep a ratio in mind: the repo already quotes everything as a signal ratio against the noise floor. Decibels are just 10·log₁₀ of that ratio, so once dB feel natural, every constant in radarGameSettings.ts reads as a real radar parameter.
